# 2026-10-10 — Experiment #1: 8B Q4_K_M on the RX 6700 XT (Vulkan)

**Machine:** SANDBOX · Windows 11 Pro · RX 6700 XT `gfx1031` (driver `32.0.21045.5002`) ·
Ryzen 5 5600 · 31.9 GB · **no ROCm** · llama.cpp `b11541` Vulkan
**Goal:** confirm a 7–8B model is a viable daily driver on 12 GB of VRAM and measure it.
**Result:** ✅ works — **8B Q4_K_M fully offloads, ~63 t/s decode, ~7.6 GB at 32K context.**

## Context

The 4B baseline left lots of VRAM free. The open question from
[`../benchmarks/2026-10-10-baseline.md`](../benchmarks/2026-10-10-baseline.md) was whether the
"7–8B Q4 fits comfortably" prediction held on real hardware. It does.

## Steps

```powershell
$dir   = "C:\Users\6700XT\llm\bin\llama-b11541-vulkan"
$model = "C:\Users\6700XT\llm\models\Qwen3-8B-Q4_K_M.gguf"   # 4.68 GiB, 8.19 B params

& "$dir\llama-bench.exe" -m $model -ngl 99 -r 3
& "$dir\llama-bench.exe" -m $model -ngl 99 -fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0
# then llama-server at ctx 8192 / 32768 with KV q8_0 / q4_0, GPU counter sampled
```

## Findings

- 8B Q4_K_M decode **~63 t/s**, prompt **~624 t/s**; fully GPU-offloaded (`-ngl 99`). `Verified: 2026-10-10`
- 32K context with q8_0 KV peaks at **~7.6 GB** of 12 GB — **~4 GB headroom**. `Verified: 2026-10-10`
- KV `q4_0` saves **~1.1 GB** at 32K for **~4%** decode loss. `Verified: 2026-10-10`
- 8B is **×0.66** decode vs 4B — sub-linear, so the 4B was not purely bandwidth-bound. `Verified: 2026-10-10`
- Flash-attention remains **neutral** at this size on RDNA 2. `Verified: 2026-10-10`
