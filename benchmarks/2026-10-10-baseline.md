## Throughput — `llama-bench`, full offload (`-ngl 99`, `-r 3`)

### 4B — `Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 Q4_K_M`

| KV cache | flash-attn | pp512 (t/s) | tg128 (t/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| f16 | auto | **1028.8 ± 1.2** | **95.3 ± 1.1** |
| f16 | on | 1029.4 ± 3.6 | 96.8 ± 0.1 |
| q8_0 | on | 997.8 ± 0.9 | 93.4 ± 0.2 |
| q4_0 | on | 994.1 ± 0.7 | 92.4 ± 0.1 |

### 8B — `Qwen3-8B Q4_K_M`

| KV cache | flash-attn | pp512 (t/s) | tg128 (t/s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| f16 | auto | **623.6 ± 0.4** | **63.1 ± 0.0** |
| f16 | on | 621.8 ± 0.6 | 63.0 ± 0.1 |
| q8_0 | on | 611.2 ± 0.7 | 60.7 ± 0.1 |
| q4_0 | on | 609.0 ± 0.4 | 60.6 ± 0.0 |

**Scaling read (8B vs 4B):** decode **×0.66** (95→63), prompt **×0.61** (1029→624). Not the ×0.5
you'd get if decode were purely memory-bandwidth-bound — the 4B is small enough that fixed
per-token overhead matters. Both are fully interactive.

**Flash-attention:** neutral at both sizes on this card. **KV quantization:** `f16 → q4_0` costs
**~3–4%** decode at 4B and **~4%** at 8B — cheap for the VRAM it saves.

## VRAM — adapter dedicated usage (`\GPU Adapter Memory(*)\Dedicated Usage`)

Measured with `llama-server` (`-np 1`, `-fa on`, `-ngl 99`). Absolute totals include whatever the
desktop was using that session, so **Δ over idle** is the comparable figure.

| Model | Config | Adapter VRAM (MB) | Δ over idle |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4B | idle (desktop/driver) | 1134 | — |
| 4B | ctx 8192, KV q8_0 | 4226 | ~3092 |
| 4B | ctx 32768, KV q8_0 | 6096 | ~4962 |
| 4B | ctx 32768, KV q4_0 | 4930 | ~3796 |
| 8B | idle (desktop/driver) | 557 | — |
| 8B | ctx 8192, KV q8_0 | 5764 | ~5207 |
| 8B | ctx 32768, KV q8_0 | 7632 | ~7075 |
| 8B | ctx 32768, KV q4_0 | 6510 | ~5953 |

**Read:** the **8B Q4 at 32K context peaks at ~7.6 GB** total — it **fits in 12 GB with ~4 GB to
spare**. KV `q4_0` saves **~1.1 GB** at 32K for both sizes. The 8B at 32K is the tightest config
tested and it is still comfortable.

Server-reported buffers (verbose load log, 4B):

| Config | Vulkan0 model buf | CPU_Mapped model buf | Vulkan0 compute buf |
|---|---|---|---|
| ctx 8192 | 2375.91 MiB | 304.28 MiB | 85.09 MiB |
| ctx 32768 | 2375.91 MiB | 304.28 MiB | 109.09 MiB |

## Observations

- ✅ **Vulkan "just works"** on `gfx1031` with only the graphics driver — no ROCm, no
  `HSA_OVERRIDE_GFX_VERSION`.
- ✅ The card reports its **true 12 GB** over Vulkan (12272 MiB), and **8B Q4 fully offloads** (`-ngl 99`).
- **bf16 is unsupported** (`bf16: 0`) and there are **no matrix cores** — expected for RDNA 2.
- This build defaults the server to **port `9931`**.
- **8B Q4_K_M is the practical daily driver** on this card: ~63 t/s decode, ~7.6 GB at 32K.

## Reproduce

```powershell
$dir = "C:\Users\6700XT\llm\bin\llama-b11541-vulkan"
$model = "C:\Users\6700XT\llm\models\Qwen3-8B-Q4_K_M.gguf"   # or the 4B file

# throughput matrix
& "$dir\llama-bench.exe" -m $model -ngl 99
& "$dir\llama-bench.exe" -m $model -ngl 99 -fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0

# serve (observe the launch)
& "$dir\llama-server.exe" -m $model -c 32768 -np 1 -ngl 99 -fa on `
  -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0 --host 127.0.0.1 --port 9931
```
